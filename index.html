Method

Several checks before one conclusion.

01

Bring the evidence

Upload PDF, TXT, Markdown, or TeX reports, an optional underlying manuscript, and optional private known-author samples.

02

Compare broadly

Check language, recurring errors, terminology, academic fit, PDF metadata, low-weight TeX habits, and broad public pre-2026 solo writing.

03

Confirm independently

Blind-confirm the leading candidates, re-test close finalists, and report a descending probability chart including an outside-shortlist outcome.
